# Architectural Rationale

Sabueso, Praxis, and Nextia provide persistent Scientific Context — knowledge, know-how, and discovery experience. MolSysSuite remains the molecular modeling ecosystem, explicitly connected to that context.

MOLI Agent is optional because Molecular Intelligence must remain possible with human scientific reasoning alone.

MolSys-AI remains inside the MolSysSuite domain because its specialization is the understanding and operation of MolSysSuite itself. MOLI Agent has a different scope: scientific reasoning across the investigation. MOLI may delegate modeling operations without making MolSys-AI a mandatory execution gateway.

MOLI's Context Assembly is distinct from conventional RAG. RAG can retrieve software documentation or unstructured information; Scientific Context Assembly preserves structured scientific semantics, provenance, conflicts, methodological validation, and discovery history.

The dynamic view ends in `LEARN`, which closes the loop by retaining experience in Nextia and, through explicit gates, promoting reusable knowledge to Sabueso and validated know-how to Praxis.

Three complementary views remain: structural, functional, and dynamic. Sabueso SourceAssertion, Nextia Evidence, and cross-cutting Provenance remain deliberately distinct.
