# Molecular Intelligence

`Molecular Intelligence` is reserved for the **capability of the complete scientific system**. It is not the name of Sabueso/Praxis/Nextia, it is not a package boundary, and it does not imply that an LLM is present.

## Definition

Molecular Intelligence emerges from combining:

1. **Scientific Context** — structured Knowledge, Know-how, and Discovery context;
2. **Molecular Modeling** — representations and computational models of molecular systems, primarily through MolSysSuite;
3. **Scientific Reasoning** — interpretation, hypothesis formation, planning, judgment, and decision-making.

```text
Scientific Context
        +
Molecular Modeling
        +
Scientific Reasoning
        ↓
MOLECULAR INTELLIGENCE
```

Scientific Reasoning may be performed entirely by human scientists or augmented by MOLI Agent.

This distinction is deliberate. The persistent scientific context and reproducible modeling infrastructure survive changes in LLMs, foundation models, agent runtimes, or other AI technologies. Artificial intelligence can amplify Molecular Intelligence without defining it.
