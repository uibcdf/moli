# Authority for external issues and pull requests

Accepted by the principal maintainer in [MolI #80](https://github.com/uibcdf/moli/issues/80).
This rule applies to agents developing MolI components and supporting tools,
including MolSysSuite members. MolI owns the platform boundary; MolSysSuite owns
its member instructions and delivery under
[suite #159](https://github.com/uibcdf/molsyssuite/issues/159).

## Prior explicit permission

Before an agent creates or publishes an issue or pull request in a repository
outside MolI's scope or not owned by us, it must obtain explicit permission from
either **Diego Prada Gracia** or **Liliana Marisol Moreno Vargas**. In particular,
every repository outside `https://github.com/uibcdf` falls within this boundary.
A remotely created draft pull request is already an external write.

UIBCDF ownership alone does not authorize writes to every UIBCDF repository.
Use the registered MolI/suite scope, provider ownership and repository-specific
contribution rules to identify the destination and authority. Working alongside
an approver, credentials, internal direct-push permission, autonomous-work
instructions or the universal feedback duty are not destination-specific external
publication permission.

## Prepare, review and publish

1. Investigate through read-only routes and record an appropriate internal finding.
2. Prepare the report or patch locally. Identify the destination repository, the
   proposed issue/PR action and the material to disclose, including attachments.
3. Present that concrete material to either named approver and obtain explicit
   permission before any external issue/PR creation or publication. Alternatively,
   hand it to the human for submission.
4. Act only within the approved destination, action and content scope. Existing
   explicit authorization for the same work remains valid without repeated
   requests; another destination or broader disclosure needs its own permission.
5. If permission is absent, retain the internal/local result and identify the
   pending human handoff. Do not bypass the boundary through a fork, another
   agent, account, tool or automation.

Read-only research and locally prepared drafts remain available. The
[reporting duty](reporting_protocol.md) still requires an observable internal
finding or authorized handoff; an external authorization boundary is not a
reason to lose the finding. Confidential or exploitable material follows the
private route and its disclosure authority. This policy grants no permission
to submit private material merely because an issue destination is approved.

## Internal contributions and adoption

Authorized internal reporting, owner review and direct-push development keep
their existing scope. The [cross-repository contribution route](cross_component_feedback.md#contributing-a-fix-across-repositories)
operates subject to the boundary above; a ready patch is not permission to
publish it externally. Provider review and consumer-impact notices remain
separate obligations.

Root contributor instructions, the canonical component guide and new-component
templates carry this action. The [adoption record](publication_policy_adoption.md)
distinguishes central policy delivery from component-owned instruction commits
and checked guide equality. No runtime or scientific certification follows from
receiving an instruction.
