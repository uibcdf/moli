# Local Discussions upkeep outline

Adapt this outline under the [Discussions policy](../governance/github_discussions.md).
Use `devguide/discussions.md`, `devguide/discussions/README.md` or a discoverable
local equivalent. Root instructions must reach it directly or through the shared
guide. Replace examples with actual local ownership and observed vocabulary.

1. Purpose, owner/team and triage/delegation route; current versus proposed setup.
2. Required Announcements, Q&A and Ideas and their formats; chosen extra categories
   with actual purposes/forms/destinations. Optional suggestions need no exception
   when omitted; preserve useful local arrangements.
3. Thread contents, search/reuse and canonical resource conversations; relevant
   versions/sources, evidence, limitations and confidentiality.
4. Actual label vocabulary, meanings, uses and application rules; if none is
   maintained, say so instead of inventing labels or claiming live state.
5. Reviews, updates/corrections, answers, duplicates and useful history.
6. Actionable issues and accepted/deferred/rejected decisions, dates/reasons,
   revisit triggers and reciprocal links to the original conversation.
7. Posting/publication authority and private/security handoffs.
8. Dated configuration observations, including rendered forms where relevant;
   guidance delivery alone does not certify settings or actual adoption.

Keep resource descriptions, arguments and reviews in useful Discussions threads.
The upkeep guide explains practice and links authoritative artifacts/decisions;
it does not require duplicating every conversation in devguide.
