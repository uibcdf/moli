# Using and maintaining MolI Discussions

Local upkeep guide under [#93](https://github.com/uibcdf/moli/issues/93), following
[the platform policy](governance/github_discussions.md). The initial activation
in #23 remains a dated completed record. This document changes no remote setting,
category, label or form.

## Where a conversation belongs

Diego Prada (`dprada`) owns platform triage, with delegation to repository
maintainers. Search existing issues and conversations before opening a new thread.

| Category | Use | Thread contents and outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Announcements | Maintainer updates about a recorded release, contract or rule | Link the authoritative record and exact scope; a thread does not create release approval. |
| Q&A | Questions about MolI policies and component boundaries | State the question, relevant version/context and existing references; mark the useful answer and link lasting clarification. |
| Ideas | Early cross-component questions without selected actionable work | State the undecided question, alternatives and useful outcome; move concrete work to its owning issue. |

Q&A and Ideas use the checked-in category forms. Follow their prompts and use
public-safe examples. This guide documents the required baseline; current remote
configuration needs separate API/UI observation, including rendered forms.

Optional resource categories are useful only where maintainers choose them.
MolI currently declares no additional category in this local guide. Suggested
names in policy are a menu, not a six-category target or a request to create them.
Resource reviews, arguments and conclusions stay in their conversations; link
reproducible artifacts or actionable decisions to their authoritative records.

## Labels and review

No discussion-specific label vocabulary is prescribed here. Before applying a
label, inspect the repository's actual labels and their maintained descriptions;
issue labels or suite labels are not proof of a matching Discussion vocabulary.
A maintainer proposing labels records the meaning, scope, application rule and
observed versus desired state. Scientific components may use different terms.

Keep a canonical conversation for a resource/topic where practical. New reports
identify the source/version, relevance, evidence and limits; distinguish a proposed
resource from a received one. Updates retain earlier reviewed scope and dates.
Link duplicates to the useful canonical conversation and explain their disposition;
preserve history. Correct stale answers explicitly and retain the reason.

Actionable findings, even uncertain ones, need an owning issue. Accepted, deferred
and rejected decisions retain date/reason and reciprocal links; deferrals name a
revisit condition. Use the existing reporting protocol rather than treating Ideas
as an unowned backlog. Closing or answering a thread is not scientific validation.

## Authority and privacy

Read-only research and summaries preserve source links. Posting, subscriptions and
disclosure require the task's applicable authority; this upkeep guide grants none.
Keep confidential science, credentials and restricted inputs out of public threads.
Use the private maintainer/security route when appropriate. Before changing
categories/forms/labels, identify the owner, useful history and the concrete action.
